Licence validity

The City's file has no expiry field. Licences run one year and renew on the anniversary of the issue date. Renewed means the record was updated in the 120 days before the anniversary. Renewal not recorded means the term ended with no renewal update yet. Confirm with 311 before relying on a status.

Websites & phone numbers

Phone numbers are not shown, to protect the privacy of sole proprietors who list a personal number. The City's data has no websites. A “Website” button and a picture of the site appear only where it has been checked that the site shows the licensed address or phone. Elsewhere “Find website” runs a web search, so check that the result is the right business.

Map locations

Each business is placed by matching its address to the City's Address Points file. Most match exactly. Some are placed at the nearest number on the same street, or at the centre of their postal code, and the card says so. Businesses with an address outside Toronto aren't shown on the map. Not listed

Licences held by individuals for a vehicle, cart, sign or donation bin, whose addresses are usually homes. Owner names appear only when the owner is a company.

Area only

Businesses licensed to an individual without business premises, such as driving instructors, show only their postal area and ward. Their licence address is usually a home, so it isn't shown and they aren't placed on the map.
