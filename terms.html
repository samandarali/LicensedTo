1. Who runs LicensedTO

2. Where the information comes from

Business details come from the City of Toronto’s open data set “Business Licences and Permits” (Municipal Licensing & Standards). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto.

We reorganise the data so it can be searched and mapped. Some fields, such as whether a licence appears renewed, due or not recorded, are our estimates worked out from the dates in the City’s file. They are not decisions made by the City.

Phone numbers listed in the City’s file are deliberately not shown, because some sole proprietors list a personal number.

Website links and website preview images are added by us by matching a business’s public website to the licence address or phone number. A match can be wrong.

  • No tracking: LicensedTO itself has no advertising and no analytics. We don’t see your searches, and we don’t sell or share information about you.
